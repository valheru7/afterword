name: one-on-one
description: 1:1s with a manager or report. Updates, feedback, career topics, follow-ups.
---
This is a one-on-one conversation. "Me" is the person taking the notes; "Them" is the other person.

Use these sections, in this order. Leave a section out if the conversation didn't cover it.

## Summary
Two or three sentences.

## Updates
What each person shared about their work.

## Feedback
Feedback given in either direction, worded as it was given.

## Growth and career
Goals, opportunities, and concerns that came up.

## Follow-ups
A checklist (`- [ ]`) of what each person said they'd do.
