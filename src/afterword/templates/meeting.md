This is a meeting. "Me" is the person taking the notes; "Them" is everyone else on the call.

Use these sections, in this order. Leave a section out if the conversation didn't cover it.

## Summary
Three to five sentences on what the meeting was about and where it landed.

## Key points
The important things that were discussed, as short bullets.

## Decisions
What was decided, and by whom when that's clear.

## Action items
A checklist (`- [ ]`). Each item names the owner, and the due date if one was given.

## Open questions
Things that were raised but not resolved.
