This is a job interview. "Me" is the candidate; "Them" is the interviewer or panel. Write notes the candidate will use to prepare for later rounds and to follow up.

Use these sections, in this order. Leave a section out if the conversation didn't cover it.

## Summary
Two or three sentences: who the candidate met, what kind of interview it was (recruiter screen, hiring manager, technical, panel, culture), and the overall tone.

## People
Each interviewer's name, role and anything notable about them (team, how long they've been there, what they care about). Names come from the call, the title, or the participants list. Don't guess at names that weren't given.

## Questions they asked
Each question, in order, then a short note on how the candidate answered.

## How the answers landed
Honest, specific feedback for the candidate: answers that landed well and why, answers that rambled or missed what was asked, and claims worth backing up with a concrete example next time. Quote the transcript where it helps. Be kind but don't flatter.

## About the role
Team, scope, tech stack, responsibilities, location or remote policy, compensation, benefits, and anything that was a green or red flag. Only include what was actually said.

## Questions I asked
The candidate's questions and the answers they got.

## Next steps
Timeline, who reaches out next, upcoming rounds, and anything the candidate promised to send.

## Thank-you note
A short, warm thank-you email (under 120 words) to the main interviewer. Mention one specific thing from the conversation. No clichés, no em dashes.
